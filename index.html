Download product brief (Word) ↗

Staffing teams prepare agreements using information spread across confirmed orders and draft documents. A different rate, missing payment term, or incorrect date can trigger another round of follow-up.

This assistant brings those terms and their source evidence together before the agreement moves to approval.

The problem

Manual document comparison takes effort, and small commercial differences are easy to overlook.

The solution

Compare the key terms, see exactly where a finding comes from, correct an extraction, record an exception, and prepare the next conversation in one place.

The value to validate

Reduce active review effort and avoid preventable rework while maintaining discrepancy detection. The value calculator shows assumptions, not measured customer savings.

What this demo supports

English labeled text, editable sample documents, source evidence, corrections, exception notes, clarification drafting, and review exports. Structured review runs locally. Live AI extraction requires a connected service and is clearly identified when selected.

Final commercial and legal approval remains with the authorized reviewer.

The staffing agreement problem

The draft does not always match the confirmed order

  • A staffing coordinator checks the draft against the order, including rates, dates and payment terms.
  • A wrong rate can mean rework now or a billing dispute later.
  • The account manager resolves questions about the terms. The authorized reviewer approves the agreement.

3 / 8

The review workflow

Each flagged term links back to the source text

  • 1. Add the confirmed order and draft agreement.
  • 2. Compare the terms and check the source text for any differences.
  • 3. Correct a misread value or explain why a difference is acceptable.
  • 4. Edit the follow-up message and download the review.

6 / 8

What works today

I kept the first version focused on checking commercial terms

  • Review labeled text, inspect flagged terms and download the findings with your notes.
  • The checks account for date formats, currencies and hourly versus daily rates.
  • The demo uses local rules. A live AI service is not connected yet.
  • PDF reading, signature routing and saved review history would need further development.

7 / 8

How I would test whether it helps

The key question: can people review faster without missing errors?

  • Compare review time and follow-up rounds with the current manual process.
  • Check for missed errors, incorrect all-clear results and whether the source text supports each finding.
  • Ask staffing coordinators to review the same types of agreements with and without the tool.
  • Test AI extraction against labeled examples before adding more document formats.

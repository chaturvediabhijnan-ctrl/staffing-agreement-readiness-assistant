04 / THE PRODUCT STORY

Download product brief (Word) ↗

Staffing teams prepare agreements using information spread across confirmed orders and draft documents. A different rate, missing payment term, or incorrect date can trigger another round of follow-up.

This assistant brings those terms and their source evidence together before the agreement moves to approval.

The problem

Manual document comparison takes effort, and small commercial differences are easy to overlook.

The solution

Compare the key terms, see exactly where a finding comes from, correct an extraction, record an exception, and prepare the next conversation in one place.

The value to validate

Reduce active review effort and avoid preventable rework while maintaining discrepancy detection. The value calculator shows assumptions, not measured customer savings.

What this demo supports

English labeled text, editable sample documents, source evidence, corrections, exception notes, clarification drafting, and review exports. Structured review runs locally. Live AI extraction requires a connected service and is clearly identified when selected.

Final commercial and legal approval remains with the authorized reviewer.

The staffing agreement problem

A confirmed order and its draft can disagree

  • Operations coordinators compare rates, dates, hours, entities, and payment terms across documents.
  • A missed discrepancy can lead to another review cycle or a billing dispute.
  • Account managers resolve commercial questions. Authorized reviewers retain final approval.

3 / 8

The review workflow

Evidence stays close to each decision

  • 1. Add the confirmed order and draft agreement.
  • 2. Compare supported terms and inspect the original source lines.
  • 3. Correct an extraction or record a justified mismatch exception.
  • 4. Edit the clarification and export the review for follow-up.

6 / 8

Product scope and decisions

A focused review experience with explicit limits

  • Working release: labeled text review, source evidence, corrections, exception notes, clarification, and exports.
  • Comparison rules handle dates, amounts, currency, and rate basis explicitly.
  • Live AI integration is prepared separately and remains unconnected.
  • PDF/OCR, signature routing, and persistent audit history are outside the current release.

Structured review works today. Live AI remains unconnected.

7 / 8

Success measures and next steps

Faster review must preserve discrepancy detection

  • Measure active review time and clarification rounds.
  • Track missed discrepancies, false-clear outcomes, and evidence validity.
  • Validate the workflow with staffing practitioners and matched review tasks.
  • Connect and evaluate live AI before expanding document formats or integrations.

# Staffing Agreement Readiness Assistant

A document-review application designed by **Abhijnan Chaturvedi** for staffing operations teams.

## The problem

A confirmed staffing order and its draft agreement can disagree on rates, dates, weekly hours, contracting entities, or payment terms. Reviewing those differences manually takes effort and can create avoidable follow-up.

## The solution

Compare the documents in one workspace, inspect source evidence, correct extracted terms, record justified exceptions, and prepare a clarification before approval.

## Try the product

[Open the running application](https://chaturvediabhijnan-ctrl.github.io/staffing-agreement-readiness-assistant/).

The Product story section includes a downloadable Word brief covering the problem, solution, workflow, business case, and success measures.

1. Choose a fictional sample or paste your own labeled document text.
2. Compare the confirmed order and draft agreement.
3. Inspect the evidence behind each finding.
4. Correct a term or record an accepted mismatch exception.
5. Review the clarification and export the session.

The application also explains the problem, solution, workflow, and potential value. Its value calculator uses editable assumptions, not measured customer savings.

## Current scope

Structured text review works in the browser without login or paid services. It uses explicit rules to read labeled fields. Live AI extraction requires a separately connected service and is not active in this release. The product clearly distinguishes these methods.

English text only. No PDF/OCR, automatic messaging, signature sending, or legal approval. Final approval remains with the authorized reviewer.

The public application is self-contained in `index.html`. Sample organizations and documents are fictional.
